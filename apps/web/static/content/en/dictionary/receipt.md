# Your Betting Receipt: Proof of Your Stake

When you place your bet on **radix.bet**, you’ll receive a **betting receipt**—your personal proof of stake. This receipt reflects the **exact amount of tokens** you’ve put into the smart-contract bet, no more, no less.

## How It Works:

1. **Place Your Bet**  
   Once you enter your tokens into a bet, the smart contract instantly issues a **betting receipt token** that’s tied to the amount you wagered.

2. **Hold Onto Your Receipt**  
   Your betting receipt is your key to claiming your winnings. It stays valid throughout the entire betting process.

3. **Claim Your Prize**  
   When the bet is over and a winner is chosen, you use your **receipt tokens** to claim whatever is calculated as your prize. The more you wager, the more you stand to win, and the receipt guarantees you get exactly what you’re owed—nothing is lost, and nothing gets skimmed.

## Your Betting Receipt is Safe

Once you receive your **betting receipt**, it’s yours and **cannot be taken away** or recalled by anyone—not even the bet creator. The receipt is securely tied to your wallet through the smart contract, ensuring full control over your bet and your winnings. Only you have the power to use your receipt to claim your prize when the time comes.

## What Happens if You Lose?

If your bet doesn't win, your **receipt tokens** lose their value for that bet. You won’t be able to claim any prizes with them. However, the process remains transparent, and your initial stake is securely processed. In short, losing tokens simply means no prize for that round, but no one’s messing with the outcome—it’s all fair game.
